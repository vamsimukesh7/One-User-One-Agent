# Known limitations

- All twelve goals have implementation support. G06 uses the user-approved 30-day lapse threshold; G10 requires two distinct episode completions on two IST days within seven days. G11 requires real TV telemetry, absent from the supplied pack: selection is disabled unless instrumentation is explicitly declared. No TV observations are fabricated.
- G07 requires a covered lifetime and valid catalogue episode IDs. Older viewers with truncated histories cannot prove an episode is still unwatched. Unknown episode references and unavailable/wrong-locale targets fail closed.
- All four channels produce local simulator-compatible actions. There are no real push/WhatsApp credentials, external sends, delivery integrations, frontend or cloud deployment. These are outside the requested local assignment scope. The simulator queues in-app messages without impression events; their individual effect stays inconclusive.
- The provider is a replay/mock advisor with deterministic fallback, not a live LLM. No personal API key is required. Custom providers must be process-safe and picklable on Windows; process startup failure and timeout fall back safely.
- Goal correction supports structured subscription, signup-window and absence predicates, known success events and explicit deadline updates. Unknown conditions or text-only changes fail closed; arbitrary natural-language rules are not interpreted as executable policy. Confirmed historical judgments are preserved; matching open plans and unfinalized failed judgments can be revised, subject to an explicit control's applicability.
- “Never,” inactivity and negative outcomes rely on complete observation of the documented window. Missing observations must be marked incomplete to be censored/inconclusive. This pack cannot prove an authoritative lifetime history for every viewer.
- Phone occurrence is estimated using buffering duration and server receipt. Unknown network latency and device-clock changes within a buffered batch cannot be repaired.
- SQLite is a single-writer local journal. WAL, bounded lock waits, transactional state, idempotency and restart cancellation are implemented. Distributed serving, remote exactly-once delivery and arbitrary mid-replay CLI resume are not. Operating-system/filesystem hangs are outside the local latency guarantee.
- `audit --at` works only for runs with the new history schema. Migration preserves existing rows but does not invent their past versions. Per-viewer facts represent their recorded last resolution, alongside later received events; they are not continuously recomputed after every irrelevant event.
- Dependency outages in the supplied pack use explicitly modeled waits. CPU/SQLite/verification time is measured separately and included in aggregate replay latency. Final timing-record bookkeeping has small additional overhead. Traffic checks are not proof of production capacity or ten-million-viewer scale.
- Batch simulator outcomes become known at the final observation watermark. They cannot retroactively affect live decisions. Production would need a continuous outcome event consumer.
- Title withdrawal blocks subsequent publication; existing remote layouts would require a platform retraction mechanism. The local verifier rechecks availability before dispatch.
- Failed-goal cooldown is a simple explicit adaptation rule. There is no fitted personalization model, calibrated success probability or reinforcement learner. Individual linked exposure/success is association, not causation.
- Impact is reported from actual randomized records even when unfavorable or inconclusive. Synthetic experiments and a single seed do not establish production uplift.
